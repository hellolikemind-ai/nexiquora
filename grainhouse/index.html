Before you book

What should we wear?

Clothes you feel like yourself in. Solid colours and soft textures photograph best. We send a short guide after you book.

What if it rains on the day?

We move into the studio on Franklin Street or pick a new date with you, at no extra cost.

How do we pay?

A 30% deposit holds your date. The rest is due on the day of the shoot, by card or bank transfer.

Can we have the raw files?

We don't hand over raw files, but every photo we keep is edited by hand and delivered in full resolution.

Do you travel?

Yes. Anywhere in the five boroughs is included. Beyond that we quote travel at cost.
