Dear neighbour

Ember & Oak started in my mother's backyard in Decatur, on a grill made from an old oil drum. We cooked the suya and jollof we grew up with in Lagos, and friends kept bringing more friends.

When the backyard got too small, we found an old brick building on Edgewood Avenue and built the whole kitchen around one fire. There is no gas line in this building, and that is on purpose.

Someone is always watching that fire. Oak goes on at four for heat, pecan later for the sweeter smoke, and by eleven it is left to burn down to embers for the next day's yam.

Come hungry, bring people, and tell us if it is someone's birthday.

Tolu Adeyemi
Chef and owner

It started with a grill

A backyard in Decatur, an oil drum and a lot of hungry friends.

Lagos food, Georgia wood

We cooked the suya and jollof we grew up with for friends who kept bringing more friends. When the backyard got too small, we found an old brick building on Edgewood Avenue and built the kitchen around a fire.

We still cook the same way: spices ground in house, meat and fish from farms we know by name, and a fire that someone is always watching.

Ask about the back room
Is there a dress code?

No. Come as you are. Most people dress like they are going somewhere nice, but that is up to you.

Can I bring children?

Yes. We have high chairs and a short kids' plate from the grill. Let us know in your booking notes.

Do you cater for allergies?

We can make most dishes without peanuts, dairy or gluten. Tell us when you book and remind your server on the night.

What if I'm running late?

We hold tables for 15 minutes. Call us on (404) 555-0142 and we will do our best to keep it for you.
