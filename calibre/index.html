    Before you join

    I've never lifted. Is that a problem?

    Most of our members started with us. Your first month covers the five main lifts with light weights until they feel natural.

    Is there a contract?

    No. Plans run month to month. Cancel before your next billing date and that is it.

    Can I switch plans?

    Yes, at any time. The change starts on your next billing date.

    What should I bring?

    Flat shoes, water and clothes you can squat in. We have chalk, belts and towels.
